## A small apartment

Back

INTERIOR

A small apartment

Small spaces often significant challenges. It was no different from the project of a small studio for my client. The apartment is located in an old tenement house. Originally it consisted of a bathroom, kitchen and living room. Our task was,above all, to separate a sleeping zone and arrange the space so that it would be functional, comfortable and beautiful.

BEDROOM

the small bedroom has been separated from the living room, with windows facing north. The new bedroom has no windows, so we have proposed a folded glass wall instead of the TRADITIONAL wall, which ideally illuminates the room and can be folded if necessary, thus opening the space. The bedroom has a double bed, a bedside table and a wardrobe. The wall is decorated with a beautiful Boudoir Navy WallArt wallpaper that goes all the way to the living room

LIVING ROOM

in the living room, we have replaced the traditional window with a wallet window, illuminating the interior. The old wardrobe has found use as a bookcase. While the armchair from the 1960s inherited from grandparents fits perfectly into the style of the apartment. The bright sofa looks beautiful against the Boiduar navy wallpaper, and wooden accessories give the interior cosiness.

KITCHEN

the kitchen is elegant and straightforward. Bright colours with wood and the green motif on the walls repeated throughout the apartment. Hanging cabinets are glazed, which gives the kitchen light and elegant touch.

## House for a family of 4

Back

INTERIOR

House for a family of 4

Large space and many possibilities. I present a house project with separate zones for the kitchen, living room and dining room.

The Kitchen

is the heart of the home. Most of the day is stay here and meals are prepared here - a place that should be unique but also ergonomic and practical. There was a small, impractical room in the designed kitchen area. After demolishing the walls and opening the space, we have a fabulous kitchen with custom-made furniture, a built-in refrigerator and an oven. Two pull-out cargo wardrobes are a perfect place for storage and the multitude of cabinets and drawers will satisfy the most demanding housewife. I combined the beautiful sea colour of the fronts with a marble top and golden accessories. The black lighting, kitchen hood, and wooden accessories constitute a comositional whole. The kitchen is comfortable and beautiful at the same time

Plants

were an essential element of interior design for my clients, so I decided to use a vertical garden in a coffee zone separated from the living room. The vertical garden is a unique element, referring to nature and cleverly placing an extensive collection of plants in one place.There is also a 3-seater honey sofa in the coffee area, two comfortable armchairs, and a table. Next to the coffee corner, there is another space:

A bright, comfortable couch with a chaise longue, a comfortable armchair and a table - this is almost everything you need in a TV corner. I placed the TV on a wall covered with old recycled wood planks. Here also a reference to nature, but not only wooden cladding on the wall is also a way to mask the lighting and cables needed to connect the RTV equipment. The whole is complemented by accessories and fabrics that make the interior cosier and elegant.

## An apartment for a young married couple

Back

INTERIOR

An apartment for a young married couple

Can an attic apartment become a dream enclave for two? Of course! I present the project of an apartment in the attic

The project included a small apartment in the attic of a tenement house. From an old, non-functional apartment, I have created a beautiful, comfortable, ergonomic interior perfect for young people. Open spaces, natural colours and materials give the interior cosiness and lightness.

### LIVING ROOM

The apartment gained the necessary space by demolishing the walls. Covering the fireplace with stone gave it a new life, and the interior gave character and cosiness. Wooden furniture and accessories and subdued colours refer to the nature and colours of the earth.

The lower cabinets are mainly drawers due to the lack of hanging cabinets. It is also an excellent solution for an ergonomic space. Dark green tiles bring colour to the interior and blend in beautifully with the cabinets and the tabletop.In the kitchen, I have also designated a small dining room.
