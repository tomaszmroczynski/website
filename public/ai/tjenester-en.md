INTERIOR ARCHITECTURE

If you dream of a completely new interior, reconstruction, a different layout of rooms, a comprehensive renovation of the house or selected rooms - I recommend INTERIOR DESIGN.

We have TWO PACKAGES in our interior design offer

### BASIC PACKAGE

a floor plan with the layout of equipment, furniture, etc.

demonstrative visualization of selected rooms OR Mood Board (colour and material board) are detailing: decorations, furniture, colours, equipment, materials, etc.

a list of interior design elements, including prices and addresses of shops/websites

2 meetings / consultations, including one with arrival at the client, inventory (dimensioning) and discussion of the project

### FULL PACKAGE

Mood Board (color and material board) are detailing: decorations, furniture, colours, equipment, materials, etc.

demonstrative visualization of selected rooms

cross-section of a selected part of the apartment

electrical installation plan with marked lighting points, switches, contacts, etc.

3 meetings/consultations, including one with arrival at the client, inventory (dimensioning) and discussion of the project

one amendment to the design (each subsequent consultation and/or modification is additionally payable).

Additional services are set individually.The package can be adapted to the client's individual needs

Decoration of Events

I specialize in the design and execution of exclusive events, including private parties, weddings, and corporate galas. With my own materials and expertise, I ensure a unique and atmospheric setting that reflects your vision. Each project is tailored with carefully selected elements such as lighting, floral decorations, and other decorative details.

### This project will include

A detailed plan for the placement of decorative elements and lighting.

A visual presentation of the decoration concept OR a plan with colors, materials, and decorative details.

A lighting plan to create the right atmosphere.

A list of specially selected decorative elements with prices and suppliers.

Three meetings/consultations, including one visit to the event venue to discuss the concept and material selection.

One revision of the design (each additional revision is an extra service).

Additional services can be customized to meet individual needs.
